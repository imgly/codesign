# youtube-thumbnail — YouTube video thumbnail

- **Size:** 1280 × 720 px
- **Aspect:** 16:9 (landscape)
- **Medium:** screen
- **Upload limit:** 2 MB — JPG, PNG or GIF; export JPEG unless the design needs transparency

## Safe zones

- **Bottom-right ~120 × 60 px:** the player's duration badge covers it in feeds and search
  (YouTube publishes no figure; guides differ by a few pixels).
- **Bottom edge:** a red watch-progress bar overlays it on videos the viewer has started.

## Notes

- Shown as small as ~168 × 94 px in sidebars and on phones — headlines need few words, heavy
  weights and an outline or shadow to read at that size.
- Building a reusable thumbnail template for a channel is the `video-thumbnail-template` skill.
