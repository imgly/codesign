# business-card

- **Print sizes:** EU 85 × 55 mm (default) · US 3.5 × 2 in (88.9 × 50.8 mm) — each +
  **3 mm bleed**
- **Medium:** print
- **Structure:** usually two-sided; side 2 defaults to a brand/logo face

## Print rules

- ≥300 dpi; fonts embedded/outlined; text inside the safe margin, out of the bleed (handbook
  §6.1.10).
- The tiny canvas makes minimum sizes bite hard: body 9–12 pt where possible, fine print never
  below 5 pt (handbook §6.1.2).

## Notes

- The smallest format in the set — restraint wins: one or two type sizes, generous margins, no
  decoration competing with the name and contact details.
