# ig-story — Instagram story / reel

- **Size:** 1080 × 1920 px
- **Aspect:** 9:16 (tall)
- **Medium:** screen

## Safe zones

Platform UI overlays the edges — keep text, logos and CTAs clear of:

- **Top 269 px** (14%) — profile, timestamp, close button.
- **Bottom 672 px** (35%) — caption, reply box, CTA.
- **Left and right 65 px** (6%).
- **Lower-right corner, 227 × 768 px** — the like / comment / share column on reels.

These are Meta's published figures for Reels and Stories on Instagram and Facebook.

**Center the content group in the safe band** rather than top-anchoring it — top-anchoring is
exactly what turns a 4:5 layout into a failed 9:16 letterbox.

## Notes

- Tall: **fill + safe zones** are the whole game. Grow type within its bands and/or open spacing
  to occupy the height; never leave the lower third empty.
