# ig-post — Instagram feed post

- **Size:** 1080 × 1350 px
- **Aspect:** 4:5 (portrait)
- **Medium:** screen

## Safe zones

- **Left and right 34 px:** the profile grid shows the centre 3:4 of a 4:5 post and cuts these
  strips (a widely used figure; Instagram publishes none). Nothing covers the post in the feed.

## Notes

- The common native format for social designs — and the usual **source** when resizing to the
  other social formats.
- Content format: no platform chrome — use the full frame inside the margins (8–12% of the
  shortest dimension, handbook §6.4).
- Feed rendering downscales; keep body text ≥ the web minimums (handbook §6.1.2).
